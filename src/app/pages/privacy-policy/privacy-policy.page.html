Brain Rush

Your privacy and data protection are fundamental to our games.

1. Overview

Welcome to Brain Rush. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitments regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data We Collect and Store

Local Gameplay Data: To provide a seamless gaming experience, Brain Rush stores your high scores, completed challenge levels, virtual coin balance, daily streaks, player name, and audio settings locally on your device via standard local storage. We do not require account registration or store your personal identity on external servers.

Technical Identifiers & Analytics: For ad delivery, crash diagnostics, and fraud prevention, third-party SDKs (such as the Google Mobile Ads SDK) may automatically collect device identifiers, including your Google Advertising ID (AAID), operating system version, and general network connection state.

3. Advertising Partners (Google AdMob)

This application uses Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as your Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data to serve personalized and non-personalized ads in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather explicit consent in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You may modify your consent choices at any time via Settings > Ad Privacy Settings within this application.

4. Children's Privacy

Brain Rush is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion policies, you have complete control over your data. You can delete all local data, coins, high scores, and player profile information at any time directly in the app by going to Settings > Delete Account & All Data.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements in our games or changes in legal regulations. For any privacy inquiries or support, please contact us at:

support@brainrushgame.com
