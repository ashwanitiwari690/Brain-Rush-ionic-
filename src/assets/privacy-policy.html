OFFICIAL POLICY

Privacy Policy for Brain Rush

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction

Brain Rush ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to safeguarding the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information, the third-party services we integrate, and your privacy choices and rights under applicable privacy laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information We Store Locally

Brain Rush is designed so you can play without creating an online account. Your progress is stored locally on your device using client-side storage:

  • Game Progress: Best scores, unlocked levels, completion badges, and daily challenges.
  • Virtual Coins: In-game currency balances earned through completing challenge levels and watching optional reward videos.
  • User Preferences: Audio volume, music toggle, haptics, and selected language (English / Hindi).
  • Player Profile: Optional player handle and avatar selected within the game.

This data remains on your device and is not synchronized to any proprietary cloud server.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Brain Rush uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display interstitial ads and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob enables us to provide the game free of charge.

To serve advertisements and protect against fraud, Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain technical identifiers, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) on Android devices.
  • IP address and general coarse location.
  • Device information (hardware model, operating system version, carrier).
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics (e.g. ad impressions, click diagnostics).

For more information on how Google processes ad data, visit Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. European User Consent (GDPR & Google UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP).

Before serving ads, users are presented with a consent dialogue allowing them to accept or customize their ad preferences. You can review or modify your consent settings at any time directly in the app by visiting Settings > Ad Privacy Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Brain Rush is created for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that personal data from a child under 13 has been inadvertently transmitted, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take appropriate corrective action.

6. Account & User Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, you have complete control over your stored data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can completely and permanently delete all game data, coins, scores, and profile records at any time by opening the app, navigating to Settings, and tapping "Delete Account & All Data". This immediately wipes all local storage.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application from your Android device also removes all locally cached data from the device.
  • Web-based Inquiries: If you have questions regarding data deletion or wish to request assistance, you may contact our support team at the email below.

7. Security

We value your trust in using Brain Rush and take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to ensure your gameplay data is protected.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage players to review this policy periodically.
